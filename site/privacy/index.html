Last updated: September 25, 2026

Ozastra LLC (“Ozastra”, “we”) is a Wyoming limited liability company. This policy explains what information we handle when you visit ozastra.com or contact us.

What this website does not do

  • It does not run analytics, advertising or tracking scripts.
  • It has no forms: nothing you type on the website is collected or transmitted.
  • Fonts, images and scripts are served from our own domain. The website does not load resources from third parties.

Hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. When you visit it, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address for security purposes. This processing is described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

When you contact us

If you write to contact@ozastra.com, we receive your email address, your name if you provide it and the content of your message.

We use this information only to reply, to discuss a potential project and to manage our business relationship with you. We do not sell it, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing. We keep it for as long as these purposes require, and as long as legal or accounting obligations require.

Links to other websites

Our website links to other websites, such as flowtopus.com. Their own privacy policies apply when you visit them.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by writing to contact@ozastra.com.

Changes to this policy
