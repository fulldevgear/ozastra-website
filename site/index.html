OUR APPROACH

How we think.
How we work.

A useful product. Data people can trust.
Both start with understanding the business behind them.

From building Flowtopus to our team’s work in enterprise data, one idea connects what we do: the solution has to make sense for the people who will use it, own it and take it forward.

01UNDERSTAND

Start with the business.
Then the solution.

Before choosing a tool or designing a screen, we look at how the work happens today. Where do people lose time? Which decisions are difficult? What needs to become easier?

Those questions give the project its direction and give us a reason to challenge a feature or an assumption when it doesn’t serve the goal.

02MAKE SENSE

Make complexity
understandable.

A purchasing decision can depend on stock, orders and supplier information. A trusted data record can depend on definitions, matching rules and clear ownership.

We work through those connections so the people using the result can understand what they see, where it comes from and what to do next.

03LEARN TOGETHER

Make it tangible.
Keep the conversation open.

A working screen, a mapped data flow or a real record often reveals more than another page of requirements. We use tangible work to make assumptions visible and decisions easier to discuss.

We bring business and technical perspectives into that conversation, explain the trade-offs and refine the work as we learn.

04THINK AHEAD

Build for what
comes after launch.

Delivery is a point in the life of a product or a data foundation. People will maintain it, question it and ask it to do more.

That is why we think about documentation, ownership and room to evolve while we build. The aim is a result your team can understand and continue to work with.

Working with Ozastra

  1. 01

    Start with a conversation

    Tell us what you want to build or improve, what is getting in the way, and what already exists. We use that first conversation to clarify the need and identify the next step.

  2. 02

    Agree on the scope

    Before work begins, we define the deliverables, responsibilities, timeline and budget together. You know what is included and which decisions need your input.

  3. 03

    Stay involved as the work takes shape

    We agree on a main point of contact and a rhythm for updates and reviews. You see tangible progress, discuss trade-offs and validate the direction throughout the project.

  4. 04

    Plan the handover

    We agree on the documentation, access and knowledge transfer your team needs. Any ongoing support is scoped explicitly.
